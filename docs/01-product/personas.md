# Personas

## Solo developer using AI agents
Needs interactive defaults, phase prompts, handoffs, and low overhead.

## Small engineering team
Needs shared repository rules, PR/Git workflow, design consistency, and traceability.

## Technical lead / architect
Needs ADRs, architecture/risk profiles, NFRs, drift detection, and change impact.

## AI-agent power user
Needs commands/skills, parallel-safe work packets, context-aware phase prompts.

## Existing-project maintainer
Needs passive scanner, current-state analysis, safe planning overlay, no forced rewrite.
